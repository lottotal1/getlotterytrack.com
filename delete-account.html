Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Lottery Track account and all associated data.

Last updated: June 28, 2026

You can request deletion of your Lottery Track account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this:

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app

This is the fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open the Lottery Track app and sign in
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) in the bottom navigation
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Your account and all data will be permanently deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by emailing us:

  • Email: admin@getlotterytrack.com
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the email address associated with your account

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 5 business days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account credentials (email and password)
  • Your profile information (name, store details)
  • All closing session records and scanned ticket history
  • All employee accounts you created
  • All store data and inventory records
  • All generated PDF statements

Retention period

After deletion, your data is permanently removed from our active systems immediately. Backups containing your data are purged within 30 days. After that point, no record of your data exists in any Lottery Track system.

Note: if you have an active subscription, cancelling your subscription does not delete your account. You must follow the steps above to delete your account and data.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at admin@getlotterytrack.com. We respond within 4 hours during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9am–7pm EST).
