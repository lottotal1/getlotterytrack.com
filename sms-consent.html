Text Message Consent

How LotteryTrack users opt in to text messages, what they receive, and how to stop.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

LotteryTrack (operated by Foundr Technologies LLC) is a mobile app that lottery retailers use to track scratch-off inventory and close out each day. We send text messages only to LotteryTrack account holders who opt in inside the app. We never text numbers we did not collect this way, and we never buy or rent phone lists.

How to opt in

  • Sign in to the LotteryTrack app and open Settings.
  • Enter your mobile number in the Mobile phone number field.
  • The opt-in checkboxes appear under the phone number. Every box is unchecked by default. Check only the programs you want.
  • Tap Save. Each program you just joined sends you one confirmation text.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of using LotteryTrack or of any purchase.

What the opt-in looks like in the app

This is the exact wording shown in the app's Settings screen:

All boxes start unchecked. Nothing is sent until you check a box and tap Save.

Confirmation texts

Right after you save, each program you joined sends one confirmation:

Welcome to LotteryTrack Missed-Close Alerts! You'll get a text if your store misses an expected close. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.
Welcome to LotteryTrack App Update Notices! You'll get a text when a new app version is available. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.
Welcome to LotteryTrack Daily Digest! You'll get up to 1 text per day with your store's key numbers after you close. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Message frequency and cost

  • Missed close alerts: only when your store misses an expected daily close.
  • App update notices: only when a new version of the app is available.
  • Daily key metrics text: up to 1 message per day.
  • Text message offers: frequency varies.

Msg & data rates may apply.

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You'll get one final text confirming it. You can also uncheck any program in the app's Settings at any time. Reply HELP for help, call (347) 716-7990, or email admin@getlotterytrack.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Read more in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
